Definition 2.1 — Categorical forward kernel
For a categorical variable x_0 \in \{1,\dots,K\}, the Markov chain
q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathrm{Cat}\!\left(x_t;\ \boldsymbol{Q}_t\, \mathbf{e}_{x_{t-1}}\right)
where \boldsymbol{Q}_t \in [0,1]^{K\times K} is the row-stochastic transition matrix, introduced by .
The matrix \boldsymbol{Q}_t absorbs probability mass into a [\mathsf{mask}] token at rate \beta_t, giving a tractable posterior q(x_{t-1}\mid x_t, x_0) in closed form.
