Same principle, very different sizes. A large language model tries to cover everything; a specific model covers one problem.
General model
Billions of parameters, trained on a large share of the public web. Answers a bit of everything.
Specific model
A much smaller model, trained on the measurements of one problem. Runs on a laptop or an embedded device.
